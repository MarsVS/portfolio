At-Home Care

Veterinary Medicine in the Comfort of Home

For pets that experience severe anxiety during car rides or clinic visits, senior pets with painful mobility issues, or busy multi-pet households, our mobile unit brings full-service veterinary care directly to you. We examine and treat your pets in their natural environment where they feel safe and secure.

  • Eliminates carrier stress, car sickness, and busy waiting room encounters
  • Ideal for multi-pet families needing simultaneous routine wellness exams
  • Peaceful, peaceful hospice support and gentle in-home end-of-life care

Why Choose Mobile Visits?

House calls offer distinct advantages for pet parents seeking personalized clinical attention:

Zero travel stress for fearful, reactive, or senior pets
One-on-one time with your veterinarian without clinical rush
Comprehensive home environment and lifestyle evaluation
Convenient scheduling that fits seamlessly into your workday
Reduced exposure to contagious illnesses in public settings

Frequently Asked Questions

What geographic areas do mobile visits cover?

Our primary service zone covers a 20-mile radius around our main facility. Extended travel outside this radius may be available for a modest mileage surcharge—contact our dispatch desk to verify your address.

What procedures cannot be performed at home?

Major surgical procedures under general anesthesia, dental cleanings requiring specialized equipment, high-grade radiology (X-rays), and intensive critical care monitoring must be performed at our main hospital. If needed, we can facilitate transport.

How should I prepare my home for the vet's arrival?

Please secure cats in a small room (like a bathroom or bedroom) 15 minutes before arrival so they don't hide under furniture. Ensure a well-lit area with a clean table or blanketed floor space where the exam can take place comfortably.
