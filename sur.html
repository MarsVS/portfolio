Frequently Asked Questions

How do I prepare my pet the night before surgery?

For most surgical procedures under general anesthesia, food should be withheld starting at midnight prior to surgery to prevent aspiration. Water is typically allowed until early morning. We will provide custom instructions for young puppies, kittens, or diabetic patients.

How will my pet's pain be managed?

Pain control is our top priority. We use a multi-modal protocol including pre-operative analgesics, local blocks, intra-operative medications, and prescription take-home oral pain relievers to ensure your pet remains comfortable throughout recovery.

When can my pet go home after surgery?

Most soft tissue procedures (such as spays, neuters, and mass removals) are outpatient procedures where pets return home the same afternoon. Complex orthopedic or major soft tissue surgeries may require overnight hospitalization for observation and continued IV medication.
