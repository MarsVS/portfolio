Proactive Pet Healthcare

Comprehensive Routine Checkups & Veterinary Guidance

Regular veterinary consultations are the cornerstone of proactive pet healthcare. Because animals often hide signs of pain or illness, routine physical checkups allow us to detect potential health issues before they become serious.

  • Complete nose-to-tail physical examination
  • Personalized nutrition, weight, and lifestyle guidance
  • Early detection of dental, skin, joint, or metabolic conditions

What to Expect

Consultation Duration

20 to 30 minutes of dedicated time with our veterinarian.

Recommended Frequency

Annually for adult pets; every 6 months for senior pets (> 7 years).

Personalized Care Plan

Tailored recommendations for vaccinations, parasite prevention, and diet.

Included in Every Routine Consultation

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should my pet have a general consultation?

Healthy adult pets should have a general consultation at least once a year. Senior pets (generally over 7 years old) or pets with chronic health issues should be examined every 6 months.

What should I bring to my pet's consultation?

Please bring any previous medical or vaccination records, details about your pet's current diet and food brand, and a list of any medications or supplements they are currently taking.

Are vaccinations included in the general consultation?

The physical consultation covers the health assessment. If vaccinations or preventive medications are due during the visit, they can be administered during the consultation (see our Preventive Care service).
