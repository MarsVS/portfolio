Preventive Care Starts in the Bowl

Proper nutrition is the cornerstone of your pet's vital health, immune system strength, and longevity. Over 50% of pets suffer from clinical overweight conditions, leading to joint degradation, diabetes, and organ stress. Our veterinary team creates tailored meal plans to keep your pet fit, energized, and thriving.

  • Precision Body Condition Scoring (BCS) and lean mass evaluation
  • Prescription diet management for kidney, liver, cardiac, and urinary conditions
  • Food sensitivity profiling and hypoallergenic trial supervision

Benefits of Optimal Weight

Maintaining an ideal weight can extend your pet’s life expectancy by up to 2 years while reducing the risk of:

Osteoarthritis and chronic joint pain
Type-2 Diabetes mellitus (especially in felines)
High blood pressure and cardiovascular stress
Skin allergies, dull coat, and persistent dandruff
Respiratory fatigue and heat intolerance

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I tell if my pet is overweight at home?

Place your hands gently on your pet's ribcage. You should easily feel the ribs without pressing hard. From above, your pet should have a visible waistline behind the ribs. If you cannot feel ribs easily or see a distinct waist, your pet may be carrying excess weight.

Why shouldn't I just cut my pet's food portion in half?

Drastically cutting standard pet food portions causes essential vitamin, mineral, and protein deficiencies. Additionally, rapid weight loss in cats can trigger hepatic lipidosis (fatty liver disease), which is life-threatening. Specialized weight-loss diets allow volume eating while restricting calories safely.

Are grain-free or raw diets recommended by veterinarians?

We recommend WSAVA-guided, science-backed diets. Grain-free diets have been linked by FDA studies to nutritional dilated cardiomyopathy (DCM) in dogs, while raw diets carry pathogen risks (Salmonella, E. coli) to pets and human families. We evaluate diet safety on a case-by-case basis.
