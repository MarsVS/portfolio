Frequently Asked Questions

When should I start progesterone testing for my female dog?

We recommend scheduling your first vaginal cytology and blood progesterone test around days 4 to 6 of her heat cycle (measured from the onset of vulvar swelling or bleeding).

How early can ultrasound confirm a pet's pregnancy?

Ultrasonic evaluation can detect fetal heartbeats and gestational sacs starting around 28 days post-ovulation. Digital X-rays are typically recommended after day 55 for precise puppy/kitten counts as fetal bones calcify.

What are signs that my whelping dog needs emergency assistance?

Immediate veterinary care is needed if she experiences strong contractions for over 30 minutes without producing a puppy, goes more than 2 hours between puppies, shows dark green/bloody discharge before the first puppy, or appears extremely weak.
