Frequently Asked Questions

Does my pet need to fast before diagnostic tests?

For routine blood work or abdominal ultrasound imaging, a 8–12 hour fast is often recommended to ensure accurate blood parameters and clear imaging, unless otherwise advised by the vet.

Will my pet require sedation for X-rays or ultrasound?

Most pets tolerate digital X-rays and ultrasound without sedation. However, if a pet is in pain, anxious, or precise positioning is required (such as orthopedic X-rays), mild sedation may be recommended for safety and comfort.

How quickly will I receive the results?

In-house blood panels and digital X-rays are completed while you wait during your appointment. Special biopsy histology or specialized reference lab tests typically take 3 to 5 business days.
