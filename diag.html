Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding pet diagnostic procedures.

Does my pet need to fast before diagnostic tests?

For routine blood work or abdominal ultrasound imaging, an 8–12 hour fast is often recommended to ensure clear blood samples and unobstructed internal imaging, unless otherwise directed during urgent care.

Will my pet require sedation for X-rays or ultrasound?

Most calm pets tolerate digital X-rays and ultrasound scans comfortably without sedation. However, if a pet is experiencing pain, severe anxiety, or requires specialized orthopedic positioning, mild sedation may be recommended for their safety and comfort.

How quickly will I receive test results?

In-house blood panels, urinalysis, and digital X-rays are completed during your appointment, with results available within minutes. Biopsy histology or specialized reference laboratory tests usually take 3 to 5 business days.
