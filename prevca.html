Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between core and non-core vaccines?

Core vaccines are recommended for all pets regardless of lifestyle (e.g., Rabies, Canine Distemper/Parvo, Feline Viral Rhinotracheitis). Non-core vaccines are lifestyle-dependent (e.g., Bordetella for dogs visiting boarding facilities, or Lyme vaccine for outdoor ticks exposure).

How often do pets need vaccinations?

Puppies and kittens require booster vaccines every 3-4 weeks until around 16 weeks of age. Adult pets generally require booster vaccinations every 1 to 3 years depending on the vaccine type and local laws.

Are indoor pets required to be vaccinated?

Yes. Rabies vaccination is legally mandated in most regions even for indoor pets. Additionally, viruses can enter homes via clothing or shoes, and indoor pets may accidentally escape outside.
