Navigating Complex Health Challenges Together

When pets present with unexplained illness, chronic symptoms, or diseases affecting internal organ systems, veterinary internal medicine provides the answers. We focus on non-surgical disease management, combining advanced diagnostics with long-term therapy to maintain high quality of life.

  • In-depth blood chemistry, hormone assays, and specialized diagnostic profiling
  • Individualized medical treatment plans tailored to your pet's lifestyle
  • Continuous monitoring and dosage adjustments for chronic diseases

When to Seek Internal Med Care

Schedule a consultation if your pet experiences persistent or unexplained symptoms such as:

Unexplained weight loss or sudden changes in appetite
Excessive thirst and increased urination
Chronic vomiting, diarrhea, or digestive distress
Persistent lethargy, weakness, or exercise intolerance
Recurrent fever or persistent unlocalized pain

Key Areas of Internal Medicine

Targeted therapeutic plans for organ systems and systemic conditions.

Endocrine Disorders

Management of Diabetes Mellitus, Cushing's and Addison's disease, and Thyroid disease (hyper/hypothyroidism).

Renal & Urinary Care

Chronic kidney disease (CKD), acute renal failure, lower urinary tract disease, and recurrent cystitis.

Gastrointestinal & Liver

Inflammatory Bowel Disease (IBD), acute/chronic pancreatitis, hepatitis, and food sensitivities.

Immune & Respiratory

Autoimmune diseases (IMHA, ITP), asthma, chronic bronchitis, and infectious system diseases.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between internal medicine and routine care?

While routine care focuses on preventive health (vaccinations, parasite prevention, wellness exams), internal medicine focuses on diagnosing and managing complex, chronic, or multi-organ medical conditions non-surgically.

How are chronic diseases like diabetes or kidney disease monitored?

Monitoring typically involves periodic lab work (blood chemistry, glucose curves, or urinalysis) every 3 to 6 months. This allows us to track disease progression and fine-tune medication dosages or dietary plans before symptoms worsen.

Can I get a second opinion on a complex diagnosis?

Absolutely. We welcome second-opinion consultations. Having prior medical records, diagnostic lab results, and imaging sent to us ahead of your appointment allows our veterinarians to review the history thoroughly before your visit.
